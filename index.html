What Does COPS Mean?

★ COPS ★

Whenever you're not sure if a website or a text can be trusted, run it through these four checks. You don't need to use them in order — think of them as four detective tools in the same kit.

C

Credibility

Can you trust who made this? Look at the web address, whether the author is named and qualified, whether there's a way to contact them, and whether their sources are identified.

O

Objectivity

Is it fair? Reliable texts stay neutral, show more than one side, and back up claims with sources. Watch out for an angry tone, one-sided arguments, or claims that seem too extreme.

P

Precision

Is it carefully made? Check for spelling and grammar mistakes, strange punctuation or ALL CAPS, and how recently it was updated. Careless texts are often less reliable.

S

Suitability

Is it the right source for your purpose? A government site is great for official facts, while a blog or interview is better for personal opinions. Match the source to what you actually need.
